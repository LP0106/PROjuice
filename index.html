About the brand

A fruit-first protein drink with a cleaner, bolder identity.

PROjuice is designed as a premium but approachable beverage for people who want breakfast, recovery or an afternoon lift without buying a heavy milkshake-style protein drink. The concept focuses on simple ingredients, recyclable packaging and a lower-waste sourcing story built around surplus or cosmetically imperfect fruit whenever possible.

The brand tone is energetic, optimistic and modern. It is built to feel at home in a gym fridge, a campus canteen, a local cafe or a weekend market stall.

Responsible by design

Performance and sustainability should work together.

PROjuice is positioned as a lower-waste beverage concept by prioritising recyclable bottles, efficient chilled batching and the use of surplus or cosmetically imperfect fruit where supply allows. That story supports both the environmental case and the emotional brand story.

Lower-waste sourcing

Use fruit that might otherwise be rejected for appearance only.

Recyclable packaging

Modern, bright bottles designed for chilled display and responsible disposal.

Smarter production

Small-batch planning reduces overproduction and protects freshness.
