    About the brand

    A premium bottle built to look modern, move fast and feel clean.

    PROjuice is not positioned like a bodybuilding shake. It is built for people who care about convenience, flavour, aesthetics and nutrition at the same time. That means the branding has to carry as much weight as the macros.

    Freshness first Real performance Responsible sourcing Premium shelf presence

    Brand pillars

    • Cleaner taste than traditional RTD protein drinks
    • Packaging designed for fridges, reels and pop-up counters
    • Lower-waste sourcing story through surplus fruit use
    • Flexible enough for breakfast, snack and recovery moments

    16-30

    Premium enough to feel elevated, accessible enough for repeat purchase.
